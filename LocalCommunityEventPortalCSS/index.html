Community Bulletin

The local community is organizing several cultural and educational events this month. Residents can participate in theatre plays, debates, elocution competitions, dance performances, and workshops. These events aim to encourage creativity, learning, and community engagement among people of all age groups. Volunteers are also invited to support event management and coordination activities. Registration for most events closes on June 15th. Participants are encouraged to register early to secure their seats.
